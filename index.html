📐 How to Take Your Measurements

Follow these simple guides to help you measure correctly before requesting your outfit.

1. Chest or Bust

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of the chest or bust. Keep it level across the back and comfortably snug.

2. Waist

Measure around your natural waist, usually the narrowest part of your torso. Do not pull your stomach in.

3. Hips

Place the tape around the fullest part of your hips and seat. Keep the tape level all the way around.

4. Outfit Length

For a dress or top, measure from the shoulder or another point specified by your tailor down to the desired hem.

💡 Remember: Keep the tape flat against the body without squeezing. Stand naturally, use inches consistently, and ask your tailor which extra measurements are needed for your chosen style.
